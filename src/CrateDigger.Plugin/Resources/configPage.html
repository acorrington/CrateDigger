CrateDigger

Describe a vibe and CrateDigger digs the matching tracks out of your library and builds a native Emby playlist.

Seed playlist trigger
Use the normal item menu on any Emby app (phone, web, TV): ⋯ → Add to playlist → your seed playlist. After a quiet window the scheduled task turns the seeds into a fresh playlist and empties the queue. Two queues exist because Emby playlists are single-type: songs → CrateDigger Seeds (audio), videos → CrateDigger Video Seeds (music videos). Status shows under Dashboard → Scheduled Tasks → CrateDigger.
Music video queue (v0.3.0)
Each added song restarts this countdown — generation starts once you stop adding. Default 60.
